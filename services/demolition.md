# Demolition

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/site-development/demolition

Demolition is usually the first step before excavation, grading and utility work. Texcon plans each job first and picks the method from there. For medium-sized buildings the most common method is mechanical demolition with heavy equipment. The page's tagline covers utility disconnects, hazard mitigation and haul-off. Part of [Site development](site-development.md).

## Types of demolition
- **Asphalt and concrete pavement:** breaking up roads, parking lots and large paved areas. Recycled material is often reused as subbase.
- **Deconstruction:** taking a structure apart to recover wood, metal and architectural pieces. Slower and more labor, but more material gets reused.
- **Foundation removal:** breaking out slabs and footings with hydraulic breakers and excavators.
- **Selective demolition:** removing one section while protecting the rest, for renovations, additions or utility work.
- **Structural demolition:** plans built from structural assessments for complex or occupied buildings.
