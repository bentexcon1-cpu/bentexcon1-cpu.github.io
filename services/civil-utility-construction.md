# Civil Utility Construction

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/civil-utility-construction

Texcon's core work: underground water, sewer, storm drainage and gas lines for municipal, commercial and residential clients, managed from concept through completion.

## Services in this line
- [Trenchless](trenchless.md): horizontal directional drilling, auger boring, pilot tube, hand tunneling and gas lines
- [Open-cut utility installation](open-cut-utility-installation.md): trenched water, sewer, drainage and septic
- [Shoring design and installation](shoring-design-installation.md): access shafts, liner plates, ring beam and sheathing, sheet pile
- [Interior joint grouting](interior-joint-grouting.md) for utility and municipal facility interiors
- [Utility line abandonment and removal](utility-line-abandonment-removal.md)
- [Roll-off dumpsters](roll-off-dumpsters.md) for utility debris and old pipe

## Choosing a method
Open-cut gives direct access for installation and repair but disturbs more of the surface. Trenchless methods go under roads, rivers, rail and structures with little surface disruption. Texcon evaluates site conditions and project requirements and recommends a method for each job.
