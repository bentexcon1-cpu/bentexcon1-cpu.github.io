# Utility Line Abandonment and Removal

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/civil-utility-construction/utility-line-abandonment-removal

Texcon decommissions old underground lines so they don't get in the way of new construction. Part of [Civil utility construction](civil-utility-construction.md).

## Two methods
- **Dig up and remove:** crews excavate, cut, remove and dispose of obsolete water, sewer and drainage pipe, then restore the site.
- **Grout fill:** for lines that are hard to reach, such as under buildings, roads or sensitive areas, the pipe is filled with grout so it can't collapse. Little excavation is needed.

## Pipeline abandonment
Turnkey pipeline abandonment, including above-ground fitting removal and right-of-way restoration to customer and landowner specifications. The page also lists:
- Small-diameter gas pipeline abandonment
- Concrete injection into pipe
- Oil tank removal, and oil storage tank closure and abandonment
- Oil spill remediation
- Facility demolition, and storage tank demolition and removal
