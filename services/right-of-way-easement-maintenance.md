# Right-of-Way and Easement Maintenance

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/site-development/right-of-way-easement-maintenance

Work that keeps utility corridors, access routes and parcels clear, safe and within the terms of land use agreements. Texcon names solar farms and pipeline easements among the sites it serves. Part of [Site development](site-development.md).

## Services
- **Brush-hogging and mowing:** rotary mowers for heavy brush, tall grass and small trees, keeping access open for inspection and repair and cutting fire risk
- **Fence gaps:** repairing breaks in perimeter fence along rights-of-way and easements
- **Mulching and grubbing:** forestry mulchers grind trees, brush, stumps and roots in place, with no burning or haul-off
- **Rock entrances:** erosion-resistant access points for heavy equipment on rural or unpaved land
- **Solar farm mowing:** keeping vegetation low around panels to limit shading, fire risk and access problems
- **Water crossings:** building and maintaining crossings over streams and canals, including bank erosion control and debris removal
