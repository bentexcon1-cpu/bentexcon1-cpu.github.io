# Heavy Haul Equipment Transport

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/logistics/heavy-haul-equipment-transport

Time-critical mobilization of heavy equipment to utility job sites. Part of [Logistics](logistics.md).

Texcon moves excavators, dozers, trenchers and shoring systems with its own fleet. The service covers route planning, permits and safety controls for the load. The aim is to get equipment on site when the schedule needs it and cut downtime.
