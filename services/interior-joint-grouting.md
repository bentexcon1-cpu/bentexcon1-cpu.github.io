# Interior Joint Grouting

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/civil-utility-construction/interior-joint-grouting

Interior joint grouting for tiled spaces inside utility and municipal facilities. The goal is joints that hold the tile, resist moisture and chemicals, clean easily and pass inspection. Part of [Civil utility construction](civil-utility-construction.md).

## Approach
- Grout is matched to exposure: sanded, unsanded or epoxy
- A set installation sequence for consistent results
- Full QA/QC documentation

## Typical spaces
- **Water and wastewater plants:** filter galleries, control rooms, headworks, chemical dosing rooms, labs
- **Municipal and transit buildings:** restrooms, break rooms, ticketing and admin areas, equipment rooms
- **Public institutions:** labs, custodial closets, food service areas
- **Support spaces:** lobbies, corridors, storage, MEP rooms
