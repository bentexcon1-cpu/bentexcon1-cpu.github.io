# Roll-Off Dumpsters

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/roll-off-dumpsters

Roll-off dumpster service for Texcon's own work and for outside construction, demolition and cleanup jobs in the Fort Worth area. The page's tagline promises fast delivery, flexible pickups and transparent pricing. Containers run from 10 to 40 cubic yards, with 20, 30 and 40 yard sizes named on the page.

## Utility construction waste
- Broken concrete, asphalt and rebar hauled to recycling plants for reuse as road base
- Dedicated containers for soil, clay and rock, and lined containers for suspected contaminated soil, sent to certified disposal facilities
- Old pipe (cast iron, ductile iron, vitrified clay, PVC) in separate containers for recycling

## Container logistics
Scheduled delivery and pickup, flexible rental periods, and help sizing the number and type of containers for the expected waste.

## Site development and demolition
- Separate clean containers for concrete and asphalt recycling
- 30 and 40 yard open tops for trees, stumps, brush and topsoil, sent to green waste facilities
- Labeled containers for concrete, metal, wood and general C&D waste to raise recycling rates and support goals such as LEED
