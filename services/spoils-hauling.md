# Spoils Hauling

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/logistics/spoils-hauling

Removal of excess soil, rock and debris from pipeline, foundation and drainage excavations, so the site doesn't get congested and work stays on schedule. Part of [Logistics](logistics.md).

## Services
- **Excavation spoil:** planning, loading, compliant transport and disposal or recycling of large volumes of earth and rock
- **Spoil removal:** disposal at authorized facilities, with recycling or reuse where possible
- **Mulch removal:** clearing wood chips and shredded brush before excavation
- **Drainage:** surface systems such as swales and catch basins, and underground systems such as French drains
