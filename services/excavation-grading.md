# Excavation and Grading

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/site-development/excavation-grading

Earthmoving that sets contours and elevations for foundations, roads, utilities and stormwater, to local engineering standards. Part of [Site development](site-development.md).

## Farm and ranch
- **Clearing and grubbing:** removing trees, brush, stumps and roots for a clean subgrade
- **Driveways and roads:** subgrade, compaction and drainage for roads that carry heavy equipment
- **Ponds:** stock, irrigation or recreation ponds, sized and sealed for the use

## Residential developments
- Grading, road networks and underground utilities (water, sewer, stormwater, telecom) for single-family, multifamily and subdivision projects
- **Drainage:** swales, French drains, culverts and storm drains
- **Pad sites:** level, compacted, well-drained building pads for residential, commercial and industrial projects
- **Retaining walls** for slopes, erosion control and level building areas
- **Septic systems:** drain field trenching, tank sizing and placement, and gravity-flow slopes

## Municipal
- **Drainage:** retention ponds, detention basins, central sewer and stormwater trenches
- **Spoils removal:** loading, hauling and disposing of or recycling excavated earth and rock
