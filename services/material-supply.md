# Material Supply

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/logistics/material-supply

Texcon sources and delivers construction materials on the project schedule and coordinates suppliers and trucking so materials don't hold up the work. Part of [Logistics](logistics.md).

## Materials
- **Cushion sand:** fine, uniform bedding sand for buried pipe. It spreads loads, protects pipe from rock and uneven trench bottoms, and helps compaction.
- **Rock and base:** including 3x5 bull rock (3 to 5 inch angular stone) for drainage, ditch erosion control, base over poor soil, driveways and landscaping
- **Asphalt millings (RAP):** recycled asphalt for temporary roads, lots, low-traffic driveways and paving base
- **Road base:** crushed limestone and recycled concrete aggregate for driveways and municipal access roads
- **Liner plate:** steel segments for manholes and underground structures
- **Water crossing culverts**
- **Debris removal:** hauling excavated earth, rock and debris to authorized facilities, with recycling where possible
