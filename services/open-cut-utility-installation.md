# Open-Cut Utility Installation

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/civil-utility-construction/open-cut-utility-installation

Open-cut installation excavates a trench to place or repair underground lines. It gives crews direct access and is cost-effective where site conditions allow it. Part of [Civil utility construction](civil-utility-construction.md).

## Municipal work
- **Drainage:** stormwater systems built for urban hydrologic conditions
- **Sewer:** wastewater lines, force mains, lift stations and manholes, with attention to grade, watertight joints and connections
- **Water:** municipal water infrastructure

## Residential developments
- **Drainage** designed around each development's landscape to keep water away from homes
- **Septic:** tanks, drain fields and piping for homes not on central sewer. The page notes that about 20% of new Texas homes rely on on-site sewage facilities.
- **Sewer:** laterals connecting homes to the main, built to local code
- **Water:** service lines from the municipal main to the home
