# Trenchless Utility Installation

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/civil-utility-construction/trenchless

Trenchless installation puts pipe and conduit underground without an open trench, so roads, rail lines, waterways and sensitive areas stay in service. Part of [Civil utility construction](civil-utility-construction.md).

## Methods
- **Horizontal directional drilling (HDD):** a steered pilot bore is enlarged with a reamer and the pipe is pulled back through. Used for water, sewer, gas, telecom and electrical lines under rivers, wetlands and other sensitive ground. Texcon runs heavy rigs for long crossings and light rigs for tight access.
- **Pilot tube:** a steerable machine drives a pilot tube between shafts for accurate line and grade, usually for gravity sewer. The bore is then enlarged for the final pipe, often by pipe jacking or sliplining.
- **Auger boring:** installs steel casing or product pipe under roads, railways and other obstacles, with the auger carrying soil out as the pipe advances.
- **Hand tunneling (manual mining):** crews excavate large-diameter or difficult tunnels by hand on complex urban sites, with ground support systems.

## Gas line installation
Residential and commercial gas line work in Fort Worth and nearby areas, across a range of pipe materials and sizes. Lines are pressure tested and checked for leaks, and the work is coordinated with utility providers and regulators.
