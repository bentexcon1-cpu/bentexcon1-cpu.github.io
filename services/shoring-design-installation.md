# Shoring Design and Installation

Service by [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md). Live page: https://www.texcon-us.com/civil-utility-construction/shoring-design-installation

Texcon designs and installs temporary and permanent earth retention for utility work, including complex underground and waterfront projects. The systems keep excavations stable, protect crews and protect nearby infrastructure. Part of [Civil utility construction](civil-utility-construction.md).

The page lists slide rail, sheet pile and trench box systems for trench safety, alongside:
- **Access shafts:** vertical shafts for utility installation, with groundwater control
- **Liner plates:** circular steel shafts that stay structurally sound and watertight in difficult soil or groundwater
- **Ring beam and sheathing:** engineered systems for deep vertical excavations and large-diameter work, fitted to each site
- **Sheet pile barriers:** interlocking steel sheets for cofferdams, excavation support, erosion control and permanent retaining walls. Texcon picks pile type (Z-pile or U-pile) and installation method for each job.
