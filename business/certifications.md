# Ownership and certification marks

[Texcon Utility](texcon-utility.md) is veteran-owned and operated. Its President, [Ben Tomerlin](leadership.md), is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran.

The footer of every page on texcon-us.com shows three marks:
- **DBE** (Disadvantaged Business Enterprise)
- **SBE** (Small Business Enterprise). The mark links to the company's profile in the SBA certification search: https://search.certifications.sba.gov/profile/H23WJLSJZAM1/92G73
- **SDVOSB** (Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business)

The site shows these marks but does not list certificate numbers, issuing agencies or dates. Anyone who needs to verify them for a bid should check with the issuing agency or contact Texcon directly.
