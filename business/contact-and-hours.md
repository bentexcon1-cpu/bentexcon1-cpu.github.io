## How to request a bid
Use the form on the contact page, choose the category that fits the work (Civil Utility Construction, Logistics, Site Development, Employment or General Inquiry) and describe the scope. The form takes file uploads, so plans, specs or an RFI can go in with the request. Texcon asks for the project location, the type of work, the target schedule and any plans on hand. Bids can also be requested by phone.

## Job inquiries
Choose "Employment" on the same form. Applications are covered in [Careers](careers.md).
