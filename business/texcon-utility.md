# Texcon Utility

Texcon Utility LLC is a heavy civil utility contractor based in Fort Worth, Texas. It builds underground water, sewer, storm drain, septic and gas line infrastructure, and it also handles the site development and logistics work around those jobs. The company was founded in 2021 and is veteran-owned and operated. Its President is [Ben Tomerlin](leadership.md), a U.S. Marine Corps veteran.

## What Texcon does
Texcon sells itself as a turnkey subsurface contractor: one company from the first site visit to the restored surface. Its work falls into four lines:
- [Civil utility construction](../services/civil-utility-construction.md): trenchless installation, open-cut installation, shoring, interior joint grouting, and line abandonment and removal
- [Site development](../services/site-development.md): demolition, excavation and grading, and right-of-way and easement maintenance
- [Logistics](../services/logistics.md): heavy haul equipment transport, material supply and spoils hauling
- [Roll-off dumpsters](../services/roll-off-dumpsters.md) for construction, demolition and excavation waste

The full list is in the [services index](../services/index.md).

## Who Texcon works for
Municipal, commercial, residential, agricultural (farm and ranch) and government clients. The site names public utilities such as municipal water, sewer and drainage, and private installations such as residential septic systems.

## Where Texcon works
Texcon is based in Fort Worth and works in 12 states: Alabama, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, North Carolina, Oklahoma, South Carolina, Texas and Virginia. The site describes this as Texas, the Southern Seaboard and the East Coast. See [Service area](../service-areas/service-area.md).
