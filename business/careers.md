# Careers at Texcon Utility

[Texcon Utility](texcon-utility.md) hires experienced tradespeople and people new to construction. It looks for a strong work ethic, a commitment to quality and a safety-first mindset.

## Roles
- **Field:** field managers, laborers, operators
- **Operations:** estimators, project managers, project engineers
- **Equipment:** equipment managers, mechanics, traveling mechanics, welders
- **Office support:** accounting, accounts payable, payroll, human resources, administrative staff

## What Texcon offers
- Competitive wages, health benefits and performance-based rewards
- Safety training for heavy civil and excavation hazards, with ongoing workshops
- A path from entry-level roles into leadership

## How to apply
Download the fillable employment application in English or Spanish from the employment page, fill it out, and upload it through the message form with the "Employment" category selected. Applicants can also call (817) 308-6464.
