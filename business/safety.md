# Safety program

[Texcon Utility](texcon-utility.md) states a goal of zero incidents. Work follows OSHA standards and local and federal safety rules, and safety officers run daily inspections and monitor work on site.

## Stated safety record
From the company's safety page:
- More than 1,250 consecutive days without a lost-time injury
- More than 500 hours of safety training completed each year
- 100% compliance with local and federal safety regulations

## How the work is kept safe
- Utilities are located through Texas811 before any excavation starts.
- Every trenchless bore, open-cut excavation, demolition and utility installation follows written protocols, daily inspections and on-site monitoring.
- Texcon designs and installs engineered [shoring systems](../services/shoring-design-installation.md) to protect crews in trenches and shafts.
- Every team member gets safety training built around excavation and construction hazards, followed by regular workshops and hands-on sessions.

## Shared responsibility
Safety officers oversee each site, and every employee is required to report hazards, incidents and accidents right away. The company calls this its Culture of Safety.

## Values
Texcon lists four core values: integrity and honesty, teamwork, safety, and professional growth.
