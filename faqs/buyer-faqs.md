# Buyer FAQs

Answers drawn from texcon-us.com. See [Texcon Utility](../business/texcon-utility.md).

**What does Texcon Utility do?**
Underground utility construction (water, sewer, storm drain, septic and gas) by [trenchless](../services/trenchless.md) and [open-cut](../services/open-cut-utility-installation.md) methods, plus [site development](../services/site-development.md), [logistics](../services/logistics.md) and [roll-off dumpsters](../services/roll-off-dumpsters.md).

**Does Texcon do public and private work?**
Yes. Municipal water, sewer and drainage, and private work for commercial and residential properties, from septic systems to large municipal projects. Farm and ranch and government work too.

**What's the difference between open-cut and trenchless installation?**
Open-cut digs a trench for direct access to install or repair a line, with more surface disruption. Trenchless methods such as HDD and auger boring go under roads, rail and waterways without a trench. Texcon recommends a method based on site conditions and project requirements.

**What excavation work does Texcon do for utility projects?**
Mass excavation, earthwork, site grading, trenching for utility installs, and specialized underground excavation for water, sewer and gas lines, from residential septic systems to municipal drainage.

**How long does a project take?**
It depends on size and scope. A small residential utility install can take a few days to several weeks, and large municipal projects can run several months. Texcon provides a detailed timeline during planning.

**How does Texcon keep excavation safe?**
Utilities are located through Texas811 before digging. Crews follow written protocols with daily inspections, and Texcon designs and installs engineered [shoring](../services/shoring-design-installation.md). See the [safety program](../business/safety.md).

**Is Texcon veteran-owned?**
Yes. President [Ben Tomerlin](../business/leadership.md) is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran.

**Where does Texcon work?**
It's based in Fort Worth and works in 12 states. See [Service area](../service-areas/service-area.md).

**How do I request a bid?**
Use the form at https://www.texcon-us.com/contact, pick the service category and describe the scope, or call (817) 308-6464. Plans, specs or an RFI can be uploaded with the request. Include the location, the type of work, the target schedule and any plans. See [Contact and hours](../business/contact-and-hours.md).
